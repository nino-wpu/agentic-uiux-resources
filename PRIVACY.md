# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy covers "Nino MCP Personal", a personal tool that connects the owner's own Pinterest account to an AI assistant through the official Pinterest API (v5). It is used by one person only: its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

## What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Pinterest OAuth, the app can read or write the following data on the owner's own account:

- Basic profile information (username, account type)
- Boards and Pins created by the owner
- Creating boards and Pins on the owner's own account, only when the owner asks for it

The app does not access other people's accounts, Pins, boards, or private data.

## How data is handled

- Data from Pinterest is used only to carry out the owner's request in the moment, for example listing boards or creating a Pin.
- OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. They are never uploaded anywhere else.
- The app does not run a public server, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and does not keep a database of Pinterest content.
- Pinterest data is not sold, shared, rented, or disclosed to any third party.
- Content returned by Pinterest may be processed by the AI assistant the owner chooses to use. That processing is governed by the assistant provider's own terms and privacy policy.

## Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them or revokes the app's access. Access can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account settings under "Apps" (Authorized apps). Deleting the local token file also removes the app's saved access.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
